Help with logging workouts, reviewing progress, Apple Health imports, backups, and optional iCloud sync.

Getting started

Open Train, select a training plan, and start your workout. Record weights and repetitions, mark sets complete, then finish the session to add it to your history.

Choose pounds or kilograms in Settings before logging your first workout.

View your progress

Open Progress to review completed sessions and the workout calendar. Select Weight progress by exercise to explore your recorded lifts.

Apple Health

On a supported iPhone, open Settings → Import from Apple Health and allow access to the information you want to use. Unlock the phone and refresh if workouts are unavailable.

Back up workouts

Open Settings → Export or restore backup, prepare a backup, then save or share it. Keep the JSON file private because it can include workout and imported Health information.

Recover a workout

Open Settings → Recently deleted workouts and choose Restore workout. RepMetric keeps up to the last 20 deleted workouts on your device.

iCloud sync

Sync requires an Apple Account signed in to iCloud. Check RepMetric Settings for sync status and use Sync now when needed. Local workout logging continues when sync is off.
