YOUR DATA, EXPLAINED

Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

RepMetric is provided by Acim Services Inc (“we”). Questions about this policy can be sent to repmetric@icloud.com.

Information stored by RepMetric

RepMetric stores workout information you enter, including exercise names, weights, repetitions, completed sets, cardio entries, workout dates, routines, preferences, and in-progress sessions. This information is saved on your device so you can review and continue your training. A separate RepMetric account is not required.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health on a supported device, RepMetric requests permission to read workouts, active energy, heart rate, and walking/running, cycling, and swimming distance. RepMetric uses information you permit to display, import, or link workout details in your history. RepMetric does not write data to Apple Health.

You can change Health permissions in Apple Health or Settings. Revoking access prevents future reads but does not automatically delete information already imported into RepMetric. We do not use Health information for advertising, marketing, or data mining.

Optional iCloud synchronization

If you enable Sync across devices, RepMetric uses Apple CloudKit and your Apple Account to synchronize saved workout information, routines, preferences, ratings, favorites, and custom equipment in your private iCloud database. Synchronized workout records may include Health information you previously imported or linked. Apple operates iCloud and CloudKit under its own terms and privacy policy.

Sync is optional and off until you enable it. Disabling sync stops future synchronization but does not automatically remove existing local or iCloud copies.

Backups and sharing

You can export a JSON backup or PDF workout report and choose where to save or share it. Backups can contain workout information, settings, and imported Health measurements. Reports can contain workout measurements. Copies you share are handled by the destination or recipient you choose. Store these files privately and delete unwanted copies from those destinations.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, RepMetric can send local rest reminders. You can change notification permission in device Settings.

Advertising, tracking, and analytics

RepMetric does not contain advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Support communications

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address and a description of the issue. We use it to respond and provide support. Please avoid sending sensitive workout or Health information unless it is necessary to explain your request.

Retention and deletion

Workout information remains available until you remove it or delete the app’s local data. RepMetric keeps up to the last 20 deleted workouts on the device so you can recover them. Exported backups may retain removed records. Uninstalling RepMetric does not necessarily remove records stored in iCloud, device backups, exported files, or support emails.

To request help identifying or removing information associated with RepMetric, contact repmetric@icloud.com. You may also manage app data through the storage controls provided by Apple for your device and iCloud account.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when RepMetric’s practices change. We will revise the effective date shown above when we do.
